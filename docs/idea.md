# Reading Together: original idea

This is the source brief for a shared EPUB reader for two people. The plan and decisions in `docs/decisions.md` work from it.

## Reading together
- Shared book library: both people can add books.
- Reading progress: "Alex is on page 42, you are on page 38."
- Sync reading mode: optionally keep both readers on the same page.
- Chapter completion: mark chapters as completed together.
- Reading streak: "7 days reading together"
- Reading goals: 10 pages a day, 1 chapter a night, etc.

## Highlight & annotation
- React to a highlight: ❤️ Love this · 😂 Funny · 😭 Emotional · 😮 Surprised · 🤔 Interesting · 🔥 Favorite · 💭 Made me think
- Add a note: "What did you think of this passage?"
- Reply: the other reader replies directly on the highlighted sentence, which makes a conversation inside the book.

## Sharing passages
- Share a passage: send a highlight to the other reader with one tap.
- Private bookmarks: favorite quote, favorite chapter, funny part, important conversation.
- Reading memories: link a highlight to a real-life memory (photo, note).

## Personalisation
Highlight and annotation colors, reader avatars/profiles, book theme, dark mode, font size, reading background, emoji reactions. Each person gets their own highlight color (🟡 you, 🔵 other reader).

## Notifications (personal, not generic)
- "❤️ Sarah reacted to your highlight: 'I love this part too!'"
- "💬 Sarah replied to your note"
- "📖 Sarah finished Chapter 5: 'Come read it with me!'"

## Spoiler protection
If one person reads ahead, their annotations stay hidden until the other reaches that page ("🔒 Sarah left a reaction on this page"), then unlock.

## Discussion questions
After a chapter, prompts like "What did you think about the main character's decision?" Both answer separately, then the answers are revealed to each other.

## Game: guess their reaction
Before the other reader's reaction is revealed, guess it (❤️ 😂 😭 😐), then reveal: "🎉 Same reaction!"

## Shared reading stats
Books together, pages, highlights, discussions, streak, favorite reactions.

## "Our Book" timeline (flagship)
A dated timeline of the readers’ experience with a book: started, highlights, discussions, shared reactions, finished. "247 reading moments saved."
