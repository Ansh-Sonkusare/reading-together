# Reading Together: original idea

This is the source brief, kept as written. The plan and ADRs work from it, so don't edit it; record decisions in `docs/adr/`.

## Reading together
- Shared book library: both people can add books.
- Reading progress: "Alex is on page 42, you are on page 38."
- Sync reading mode: optionally keep both readers on the same page.
- Chapter completion: mark chapters as completed together.
- Reading streak: "7 days reading together ❤️"
- Reading goals: 10 pages a day, 1 chapter a night, etc.

## Highlight & annotation
- React to a highlight: ❤️ Love this · 😂 Funny · 😭 Emotional · 😮 Surprised · 🤔 Interesting · 🔥 Favorite · 💭 Made me think
- Add a note: "This reminds me of you."
- Reply: the partner replies directly on the highlighted sentence, which makes a conversation inside the book.

## Couple features
- "This reminded me of you": send a highlight to your partner with one tap.
- Private bookmarks: first quote we both loved, favorite chapter, funny part, important conversation.
- Couple memories: link a highlight to a real-life memory (photo, note).

## Personalisation
Highlight and annotation colors, couple avatar/profile, book theme, dark mode, font size, reading background, emoji reactions. Each person gets their own highlight color (🟡 you, 🔵 partner).

## Notifications (personal, not generic)
- "❤️ Sarah reacted to your highlight: 'I love this part too!'"
- "💬 Sarah replied to your note"
- "📖 Sarah finished Chapter 5: 'Come read it with me!'"

## Spoiler protection
If one person reads ahead, their annotations stay hidden until the other reaches that page ("🔒 Sarah left a reaction on this page"), then unlock.

## Discussion questions
After a chapter, prompts like "What did you think about the main character's decision?" Both answer separately, then the answers are revealed to each other.

## Game: guess their reaction
Before a partner's reaction is revealed, guess it (❤️ 😂 😭 😐), then reveal: "🎉 Same reaction!"

## Couple reading stats
Books together, pages, highlights, discussions, streak, favorite reactions.

## "Our Book" timeline (flagship)
A dated timeline of the couple's experience with a book: started, highlights, discussions, shared reactions, finished. "❤️ 247 memories created."
